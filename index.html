What is Mental Health?

Introduction to Mental Health

Mental health problems encompass the mind and emotional wellbeing of people across all ages. According to the World Health Organization (2025), more than 1 billion people worldwide, or approximately one in eight individuals, are living with a mental health disorder.

We have chosen this social issue because we have seen how people suffer from these mental health problems. By choosing this topic, we hope more people are widely aware of what a mental health problem is, as well as its causes and effects. This may also help motivate them with consistency and faith to become a better version of themselves so that they may attain peace and happiness.

Effects of Mental Health

Sleep Disruption
Mental health issues can disrupt sleep patterns and lead to insomnia.
Relationship Strain
Emotional instability can cause stress in personal relationships.
Reduced Productivity
Work and study performance can suffer due to mental health challenges.
Substance Use
Some may turn to drugs or alcohol to cope with mental health struggles.
Cognitive Impairment
Focus, memory, and decision-making can be negatively affected.
Emotional Instability
Mood swings, irritability, and anxiety can interfere with daily life.
Economic Strain
Mental health problems can lead to financial stress and work loss.

Understanding Risk and Protective Factors

Causes of Mental Health Problems

Genetic Factors
Mental health conditions can run in families.
Trauma
Experiences like abuse or accidents can trigger mental health issues.
Stress
Chronic stress from work or life events affects emotional wellbeing.
Social Isolation
Lack of support and loneliness can worsen mental health.
Physical Illness
Chronic disease or pain can increase vulnerability to mental health problems.
Substance Abuse
Alcohol and drug use can trigger or worsen mental health conditions.
Economic Pressure
Financial insecurity and unemployment can contribute to stress and anxiety.
Environmental Factors
Living conditions, community safety, and pollution can affect mental health.

Mental Health Awareness

Anxiety
Racing thoughts, overthinking, constant worry.
Depression
Persistent sadness, emotional numbness, low energy.
Social Pressure
Fear of judgment, comparison, hiding emotions.
